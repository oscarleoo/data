Grenada Real Estate

One of the primary investments made in order to access the CBI Grenada programme is through Grenada real estate. Due to the growing tourism industry there is a rising demand in the tourism industry for new developments.
 
We have a list of current approved projects which is often changing as newly approved projects become available. The approved projects are fantastic opportunities for investors looking for trustworthy projects to take part in. Check the list to see what new opportunities are available.
 

Approved Real Estate Projects

To qualify for citizenship of Grenada under the real estate option, the Government requires an investment in designated real estate with a minimum investment of USD 350,000.
 
Below we list a number of Government approved projects which have qualified for this investment programme. These opportunities are often changing so please check back to keep up to date.
