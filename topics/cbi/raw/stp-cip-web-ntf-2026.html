Donation to the National Transformation Fund
A Citizenship Pathway with National Impact
Investments in the São Tomé & Príncipe Citizenship by Investment Program are allocated to the National Transformation Fund.
Once an applicant receives approval-in-principle, they are required to complete the minimum donation to the fund. This contribution is made in accordance with government regulations and forms part of the final stage of the citizenship process.
By contributing to the National Transformation Fund, applicants actively support a brighter future for the citizens of São Tomé & Príncipe.
National Transformation Fund Initiatives

Common questions about the São Tomé & Príncipe Citizenship by Investment Program.

Application Process
São Tomé & Príncipe
1. What is the São Tomé and Príncipe Citizenship by Investment Program?
It is a government-endorsed program that allows eligible individuals and families to obtain citizenship through a qualifying investment.
2. Who can apply for the program?
Principal applicants must be at least 18 years, have no criminal record and have good financial standing.
3. Can family members be included in one application?
Eligible dependents may be included in one application including spouse, de facto partners, children up to 30 years and parents/grandparents from 55 years old.
4. Is the program legal and government-authorized?
The program is safeguarded by its legal basis under Nationality Law No. 07/2022 and Decree-Law No. 07/2025, as it is duly established and governed under national law.
5. Is physical residency required?
No physical residence or relocation is required during or after the application process.
6. Can I add future dependents or pass citizenship to future generations?
Yes. Eligible future dependents may be added, subject to applicable fees and due diligence. Citizenship may also be passed to future generations in accordance with the national law.
1. What investment options are available?
The program offers citizenship through a contribution to the National Transformation Fund.
2. Can the application be sponsored?
Yes, the application can be sponsored. However, a due diligence fee for the benefactor will apply of USD 5,000, and the benefactor must provide the required documentation.
3. Does the investment support national development?
Yes. Contributions go directly into the National Transformation Fund, which supports infrastructure, social, and economic development projects.
1. How do I begin an application?
Applications must be initiated through a licensed Marketing Agent authorized by the program.
2. How long does the application process take?
Processing timelines are efficient, with a typical approval time of approximately 2 months, and approval to passport in approximately 3-4 months.
3. What documents are required?
Required documents generally include identification, background information, and supporting financial records.
4. Is due diligence conducted on applicants?
Yes. All applicants undergo comprehensive due diligence checks to ensure program integrity.
1. Where is São Tomé and Príncipe located?
São Tomé and Príncipe is a sovereign island nation located in the Gulf of Guinea, off the west coast of Central Africa.
2. Is São Tomé and Príncipe politically stable?
Yes. The country is recognized for its long-standing political stability and peaceful governance.
3. What languages are spoken in São Tomé and Príncipe?
Portuguese is the official language, with several local languages also spoken.
4. Is São Tomé and Príncipe part of any international organizations?
Yes. The country is a member of the Community of Portuguese Language Countries (CPLP).
