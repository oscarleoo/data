Contribution

The Nauru Economic and Climate Resilience Citizenship Program offers a direct and impactful contribution pathway through the Nauru Treasury Fund, supporting both the investor and the nation's development. Contributions fund projects that drive economic growth and enhance climate resilience, including initiatives focused on infrastructure and economic diversification. This unique program allows participants to support Nauru’s sustainable future while advancing their global citizenship goals, contributing to the resilience of both Nauru and the broader Pacific region.

Contribution:

  • Single Principal Applicant: USD 105,000
  • Family of up to four members: USD 110,000
  • Family of five or more members: USD 115,000
  • For each sibling of a Principal Applicant or their spouse: USD 15,000

Application Fee:

  • Single Principal Applicant: USD 25,000
  • Family of up to four members: USD 27,500
  • Family of five or more members: USD 30,000

Due Diligence Fee:

  • Principal Applicant: USD 10,000
  • Each additional dependent, aged 16 or above: USD 7,500

Other:

  • Passport Fee: USD 500 per passport

Processing Time

The program prides itself on a streamlined and efficient application process.

Total Processing Time:

  • Typically, three to four months from submission to citizenship approval

Transparent Process:

  • Clear communication and updates throughout your application journey

Expert Guidance:

  • Support from approved Agents at every step
