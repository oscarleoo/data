What is Investor Visa for Italy?

It is a 2-year visa for non-EU citizens who choose to invest in strategic assets for Italy’s economy and society.

Check our guidelines, and read more on how to apply (**)

(**) Notice is hereby given of the suspension of the program for Russian and Belarusian citizens as per the order of the Chairman of the Committee dated 07/14/2023 in compliance with Recommendation C (2022) 2028 of the European Union

(**) Following the MAECI note of 20/03/2024 implementing the EU Recommendation C (2022) 2028, we inform about the suspension of the Programme "Investor Visa for Italy" for non-EU citizens with double passport of which one is Russian or Belarusian
