Visiting Zimbabwe
Summary
 
Visas No visa is required from the majority of Commonwealth countries.Visas should be applied for in advance of arrival - from Zimbabwe High Commissions, Trade Missions or Embassies, or the Department of Immigration in Harare. Visas are also issued at the point of entry.

 

Single entry visa US$30.00 or equivalent
Double entry visa US$45.00 or equivalent
Multiple entry visa US$55.00 or equivalent
Departure Tax US $ 20.00
Travel -Air Harare Airport - International and Domestic Flights. Bulawayo, Kariba, Hwange, Victoria Falls.
Travel - Road and Rail Most major business and tourist centres are connected by first class road and rail links.
Currency Regulations Foreign Visitors are able to bring an unlimited amount of foreign currency into the country .Currency may be exchanged at most banks and hotels.
Hotel Accommodation Non-residents are required to pay hotel bills in foreign currency. The Reserve Bank of Zimbabwe requires non-residents to pay hotel accommodation accounts using foreign currency (foreign banks drafts and credit cards are acceptable). Z$ cannot be accepted by a hotel even if exchanged from foreign currency at a commercial bank or hotel.
Customs Goods up to US $ 250  may be imported duty-free once in 30 days (excluding personal possessions not intended for resale).
Motor Vehicles All vehicles must be insured against third party risks. Registration papers and drivers licences must be carried at all times.
Drive on the left.
International Car Hire Agencies are available in major cities.
Health Generally a high standard of health but contact your local doctor for information on vaccinations and malaria prophylactics.

 

Banking and Finance Services

Zimbabwe's banking and financial infrastructure is highly developed.

The Reserve Bank of Zimbabwe is the Central Bank and carries out functions normally associated with such institutions.
There are over six  major international commercial banks currently operating in Zimbabwe with over 194 branches, sub-branches, agencies and correspondence relationships with international banks.Additional banks are opening.
A number of merchant banks and discount house provide the usual financial services such as medium-term loan finance, foreign market transactions, equity issues, stock exchange transactions, portfolio management, company brooking, corporate restructuring, economic services and project finance advice.
The banking sector has been deregulated and interest rates are now market-determined. The reforms have introduced dynamism to the banking sector and new services and financial instruments have emerged. Commercial Banks have introduced international credit cards in line with international banking practice. Bureau dechanges are now operational where foreign exchange is sold and bought. Banking regulations are being reviewed to encourage greater competition in the financial sector.
Due to the complete eradication of the administrative allocation of foreign currency the banks are now the only source of foreign currency for invisible payments by individuals, parastatals, and private companies and for the import of goods by these entities.
 
The Zimbabwe Stock Exchange (ZSE) lists local industrial and mining companies and is listed on the International Finance Corporation Global Index which monitors performance of emerging markets. The ZSE is open to foreign investors. Broking firms offer comprehensive advisory services on local and foreign markets.
 
The Zimbabwe Credit Insurance Corporation underwrites political risk and commercial risk. Other risks underwritten by the corporation include insolvency of buyer, non-payment of undisputed debt by buyers and failure or refusal by the buyer to accept delivery of the goods.
Over 60 direct insurers, professional reinsurers and insurance brokers operate life and non-life funds.
 



Company Registration

The Companies Act provides for the incorporation, management, administration and winding up of companies. A company incorporated outside Zimbabwe may carry out its activities in Zimbabwe without having to form a separate a separate locally registered company. Foreign companies are required to obtain a certificate from the Ministry of Justice, Legal and Parliamentary Affairs.
All applications for the registration of a company have to be accompanied by Memorandum and Articles of Associations or equivalent documents which have to be lodged with the Registrar of Companies. The registration fee depends on capital issued, but the minimum is Z $230.
Applicants also have to inform the Registrar of Companies of the directors and persons responsible for the management of the business and their business addresses.
Eighteen months after registration, the company is required to submit statutory returns and thereafter, returns every 15 months.
 
A foreign company is obliged to maintain proper accounting records of its transactions in Zimbabwe. A balance sheet and profit and loss account must be lodged each year with the Registrar of Companies.
The Patents Act makes provision for the protection of inventions together with assignments. Licences under a patent have a term of 20 years. The protection of the patent starts on the day the patent is lodged. It costs Z$550 to register a patent.

Employment Permits

Immigration Regulations provide for companies to engage expatriate staff under temporary work permits.

Permits are issued by the Department of Immigration.

People are only permitted to work within the capacity for which the permit was issued. Spouses and minor children may reside in Zimbabwe provided they do not take up employment.

Residence Permits

These are issued to people who satisfy the following criteria:


An investor who injects not less than US$ 1 000 000  in a project
approved by the Investment Centre will qualify for permanent
residence.

An investor who injects not less than US$ 300 000 in a sole business
venture will qualify for a three year residence permit. Extension for an
indefinite period will be granted at the end of the three years subject to
the project proving viable.
A man or woman can be granted a permanent resident permit if they
continue to be married to a Zimbabwean resident for 10 years.
Dependants of Zimbabwe residents:
this applies to spouses and legal minor children of persons who acquire
residence by virtue of their having met the criteria outlined above.
Persons with professional or technical skills who invest a minimum of
US$ 100 000 in a ventre of his/her profession e.g. doctor, dentist,
architect, electrical engineer will qualify for a three year permit.
