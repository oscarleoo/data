Own your property and enjoy the benefits

Privileges granted to non-Qataris ownership

We divide the privileges into two categories as follows:

Real estate residence

First class

A property is purchased for a minimum value of 3,650,000 Qatari Riyals (equivalent to 1,000,000 US Dollars)

Second class

A property is purchased for a minimum value of 730,000 Qatari Riyals (equivalent to 200,000 US Dollars)

Ownership and Usage Benefits

  • Investment opportunities are open to Qataris and non-Qataris to own shops and offices in malls and residential complexes.
  • Permanent residency benefits are available for properties valued at no less than 3,650,000 Qatari Riyals (equivalent to 1 million US Dollars), which include benefits related to health, education, and investment.
  • Residency tied to property is granted for properties valued at 730,000 Qatari Riyals (equivalent to 200,000 US Dollars).
  • Opportunities exist to own units in residential complexes across all regions of the country.
  • Additionally, a one-stop window is provided for completing all procedures and obtaining the associated privileges.

Real Estate Sector Advantages in Qatar

  • Ranked first globally in ease of real estate property registration.
  • Ownership documents and architectural plans can be obtained in less than a day.
  • Qatar boasts the lowest real estate registration fees in the region.
  • Adoption of an electronic system for building permits.
  • Ease of transitioning between real estate projects.

Investor protection

The State of Qatar offers a comprehensive set of incentives to support investors throughout their business journey in the country, starting from the application stage of establishment to the start-up and expansion of the business activity. The Qatari government is working to create a supportive and modern business environment that supports economic freedom and rewards success by developing a comprehensive set of commercial policies that are specifically designed for this purpose.
