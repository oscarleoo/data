Golden Residency for Foreigners Owning Real Estate in Bahrain

Home / Services / Visas & Residence Permit / Residence Permits Services / Golden Residency / Golden Residency for Foreigners Owning Real Estate in Bahrain
Golden Residency
  • Golden Residency for Retired Nonresident Foreigners
  • Golden Residency for Foreigners Owning Real Estate in Bahrain
  • Golden Residency for Talented Individuals
  • Golden Residency for Foreign Residents in Bahrain (Employees/Retirees)
  • Enrolment for Golden Residency

Description of Service

Golden Residency issuance service is provided to foreigners owning real estate in Bahrain, within applicable rules and regulations of the Kingdom of Bahrain

Required Terms of the Service

  • Data validation 
  • Attach all required documents

Procedure

  •  Apply through eGovernment official website
  • Pay the application fee(s) and get the receipt

Service Channels

  • eGovernment official website 

Processing Time

  • 5 working days

Required Documents

  • Clear copy of the applicant's passport valid for more than 6 months
  • Clear copy of valid medical insurance certificate issued in the Kingdom of Bahrain
  • Clear copy of ownership title deed with property value not less than 130,000 BHD
  • Clear copy of previous residence permit (if applicable)
  • Clear copy of applicant's 6-month bank statement
  • The administration has the right to request any additional necessary documents and information at any time 

Fees

  • Application fee 5 BHD (Non-refundable)
  • The Golden Residency is  a permanent residency, subject to a standard administrative fee of BHD 300 every 10 years.

Notes

  • Payment must be made within 30 days of application approval or the application will be automatically cancelled
