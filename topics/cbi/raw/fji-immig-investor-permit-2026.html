Investor Permit

Important

  • All applications are to be lodged online through the Immi Hub portal as per the checklist below
  • All payments are to be made online through MPAISA, Visa card, Master card
  • All updates on the application can be viewed through the portal
  • Dependents are strictly limited to legal spouses and children under the age of 18. De-facto partners and children over 18 years of age who wish to reside with the principal applicant must lodge for a separate residence permit under the co-extensive category.
  • Any family member who are part of the shareholding structure or office holder should apply for a separate Investor Permit.
  • Source of funds should be legitimate and in accordance to the laws of Fiji.
  • An investor may engage in multiple ventures on the strength of a valid Investor Permit. To consolidate all shareholding company names onto a single permit, he/she must submit a new investor permit application listing all business entity details and documentations for which he/she wants to hold a valid permit.
  • Foreign company representatives or directors must submit a written power of attorney confirming their appointment
  • Any documentation that requires an English translation must be translated and Notarized from their country of residence.

Overview and description

Summary: In accordance to Section 39 to 41 of the Immigration Regulation 2007, the Investor Permit is granted to a non-citizen who is a foreign investor and/or representative or Director appointed by a foreign Company that hold shares in a locally registered company. The foreign investor is required to register its business/company and/or appear on the shareholding structure of company formally registered with the Registrar of Companies.

Requirements

Criteria

  • The Ministry reserves all rights to acquire additional documents for further verification, as and when required;
  • Where a question in a particular section does not apply to you, please put N/A;
  • Providing false information in this application can lead to imprisonment or a fine or both. Under the Immigration Act 2003 Part 2 Section 5(4)(b) – Providing false information in this application can lead to imprisonment or a fine or both.

Conditions

  • The applicant/foreign company should provide proof of sufficient funds of $300,000.00 and its accessibility to meet the proposed share contribution for each shareholder/partnership or business owners or provide proof of waiver approval from Ministry of Trade.
  • Be medically fit to invest/reside in Fiji
  • No adverse police record
  • Meet the investment threshold of $300,000.00

Validity: The permit is approved for three (03) years in principle. One (01) year is issued initially and the balance issued after lodgment of balance of investor permit, 30 days before the expiry of the first issuance.

Who can apply

  • Applicant must be made by persons over 18 years of age.
  • Applicant should be a non-Fiji citizen
  • Should be a shareholder with the name appearing in the shareholding structure, owners for sole trading business, Partnership (Partnership Agreement) and Directors or Owners Representative of a foreign company.
  • Visitor Permit holders who declare Investment upon arrival into Fiji.

Who cannot apply

  • Applicant who is a Fiji citizen.
  • Applicant who is a non-Fiji citizen and below 18 years of age
  • The persons whose details are not in the shareholding structure as shareholders
  • Applicants who hold study, internship, research, residence or visitor permit that declare holiday or visiting family and friends upon arrival
  • Applicants on Business Visitor’s Permit (BVP)
  • Prohibited Immigrants (PI) – Overstayers [Immigration Act 2003 Part 4 Section 13 (2)(a)] and Convicted Persons from any foreign country [Immigration Act 2003 Part 4 Section 13 (2)(e)]

Required documentation

Documents that must be gathered before starting the application:

  • National Police report for the applicant from the country of residence where he/she has lived for twelve (12) months or more
  • Medical report should be valid for 3 months from the date of issue upon lodgment
  • Passports must have a minimum of 6 months validity remaining
  • Passport photo must be taken within 3 months prior to the application date
  • Any documentation that requires an English translation must be translated and Notarized from their country of residence
  • All documents are to be certified, notarized by a Notary Public, Commissioner of Oaths or Lawyer or attested by the respective country’s Ministry of Foreign Affairs.

Checklist

Principal/Main applicant

  • Certified Passport Biodata Page
  • Certified Passport Photo
  • Offshore Bank Statement or Proof of investment
  • Company Request Letter – Justification for the need of Investment in Fiji.
  • Certified/notarized National Police Report
  • Medical Report
  • Investor Permit Terms and Condition – Terms-and-Conditions_Investor.pdf
  • RBF letter on issuance of shares for shareholders
  • Partnership Agreement for those in Partnership business
  • Company/Business Profile from Registrar of Companies
  • Company TIN Registration from FRCS
  • Company Registration Certificate
  • Endorsement from any Relevant Authorities

Dependents

  • Certified copy of passport photo
  • Certified/notarized national Police report (spouse)
  • Medical report
  • Certified copy of Passport bio-data page
  • Residence Terms and Conditions for each dependent – Download here
  • Marriage Certificate (spouse)
  • Birth certificate (all dependents)
